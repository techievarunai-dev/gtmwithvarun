1. The Aeronautical Science Heritage

My background is in Aeronautical Science (B.Sc., Gold Medallist, GPA 4.0). In aviation, every system is designed around a non-negotiable truth: failure is not an option, and unexpected edge cases must be caught by deterministic fail-safes.

When aircraft instruments experience sensor telemetry dropouts, redundant cross-check loops reconcile data. When a hydraulic line pressure drops, secondary bypass valves engage instantly without human intervention.

When I transitioned into Go-to-Market systems, I discovered that modern B2B revenue infrastructure suffers from the exact opposite design philosophy:

  • Silent Failure Modes: Webhooks fail silently, leaving high-value enterprise leads stranded in CRM dead zones for days.
  • Undefined State Transitions: SDRs and automated tools fire conflicting emails to the same prospect because there is no single source of truth or idempotency check.
  • High-Latency Lead Decay: Inbound prospects routinely wait 4+ hours for SDR qualification, causing conversion rates to collapse by over 70%.

I apply aeronautical engineering rigor to revenue operations. Revenue workflows should operate like avionics: deterministic, redundant, self-healing, and fully observable.

2. Three Tenets of Deterministic GTM Engineering

I build revenue systems according to three strict engineering tenets:

⚙️
Tenet 1: Single Source of Truth & Idempotency

Every prospect lifecycle stage must be governed by a centralized state machine. Before any sequence dispatch or CRM update occurs, an automated gate verifies that no conflicting touch has occurred, eliminating duplicate touches mathematically.

🛡️
Tenet 2: Self-Healing Fault Tolerance

External APIs (Apollo, Clay, OpenAI, HubSpot) will experience rate limits and 5xx errors. Every workflow must include exponential backoff retries, dead-letter queues, and automated fallback routing to guarantee zero dropped payloads.

⏱️
Tenet 3: Speed-to-Lead as an Engineering SLA

Inbound lead response must not depend on human calendar availability. High-intent demo requests must be enriched, scored against structured ICP rubrics, and synced to CRM in sub-3-minute automated SLAs.
