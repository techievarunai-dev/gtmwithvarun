CASE 03 / 06 CLIENT ENGAGEMENT

Lead Response OS

Reduced lead response time from 4 hours to under 3 minutes

The Problem

Inbound leads were manually reviewed, enriched, and routed, creating an average response delay of four hours and reducing speed-to-lead performance.

Architectural Design Decision

Engineered a webhook-driven workflow that enriches incoming leads using Clay, classifies intent with OpenAI, validates data through JSON schemas, and synchronizes qualified records directly into HubSpot.

Outcome (Validated Client Impact)

Reduced lead response time from 4 hours to under 3 minutes, increased routing accuracy, and saved approximately 28 hours of manual work every week.
