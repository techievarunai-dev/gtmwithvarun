⚠ THE BOTTLENECK

The Problem: Manual Prospect Qualification & Reply Latency

Outbound operations in commercial insurance often suffer from slow response times, fragmented qualification criteria, and manual CRM entry bottlenecks.

  • ⚠Manual prospect vetting took SDRs 15+ minutes per account, leading to low outbound velocity.
  • ⚠Reply handling was ad-hoc with positive leads waiting up to 48 hours for qualification and calendar booking.
  • ⚠High rate of email bounces and uncoordinated multi-touch touches caused domain reputation risks.
⚙ SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The Engineered Solution: 4-Workflow Deterministic Architecture

Architected a resilient 4-workflow system in n8n featuring strict schema validation, state-driven progression, and AI-powered reply triage.

NODE 01 Workflow 1: Prospect Ingestion & ICP Qualification

Apollo API queries pull verified commercial insurance decision-makers, validating employee headcount, revenue, and state licensing.

NODE 02 Workflow 2: Deterministic State Store (Google Sheets)

Acts as the single source of truth with strict idempotent updates: if a contact is already queued or unsubscribed, next touch is suppressed.

NODE 03 Workflow 3: Multi-Touch Personalized Sequencing

Dispatches staggered email touches via Gmail with tailored policy pain points, maintaining sub-minute delay intervals.

NODE 04 Workflow 4: AI Reply Classification & SLA Escalation

OpenAI GPT-4o analyzes incoming reply sentiment (Positive / Objection / Unsubscribe / Out of Office) and triggers real-time alerts.
