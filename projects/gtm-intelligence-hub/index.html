⚠ THE BOTTLENECK

The Problem: Cold Outreach Without Active Buying Signals

Generic outbound spray-and-pray generates abysmal 1-2% response rates because reps reach out without contextual timing or intent data.

  • ⚠Reps spent 60% of their workday searching LinkedIn and job boards for intent triggers.
  • ⚠HubSpot CRM records lacked dynamic technographic data and buying velocity indicators.
  • ⚠High-value signals (e.g., VP Sales hire, RFP launch) were detected days or weeks too late.
⚙ SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The Engineered Solution: Signal Listener + Multi-Provider Enrichment

Built an autonomous pipeline that listens for live signals, scores intent with GPT-4, enriches account context via Clay, and pushes enriched records straight to HubSpot and Slack.

NODE 01 Signal Ingress & Event Detection

Webhooks and API pollers monitor hiring announcements, tech stack changes, and funding events across target accounts.

NODE 02 OpenAI Intent Scoring Node

Custom system prompt evaluates account-to-ICP fit, urgency, and budget authority, generating an objective 0-100 score.

NODE 03 Clay Multi-Provider Enrichment

Cascades data across Clearbit, Apollo, and LinkedIn to enrich company size, tech stack, and executive contact emails.

NODE 04 HubSpot Upsert & Slack Alert

Updates CRM records in 118ms and sends an urgent dossier to SDR Slack channels with personalized opening angles.
